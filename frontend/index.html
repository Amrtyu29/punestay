      Help

      1. Set a location. Tap the map, press the locate button, search an address or PIN code, or type coordinates.
      2. Choose PG or hostel and who it is for.
      3. Pick a search type: within a radius, or the nearest N.
      4. Press Scan. The ring sweeps and the nearest places appear on the map and in the list.

      Within a radius asks PostGIS for every matching PG or hostel whose distance on the WGS84 ellipsoid is at most the radius (ST_DWithin on a geography column), ordered by distance, capped by the maximum results.

      Nearest N ignores the radius and returns the N closest matches using the index-assisted distance operator, so even a quiet area returns something.

      Distances are in metres along the earth's surface, not in degrees.

      Choose a basemap from the Layers panel. Overlays include the result markers, the search ring, a heatmap of results, every PG and hostel published by GeoServer as WMS, and the bus stops and metro stations around.

      Open a place to see its nearest bus stop, metro, mess, hospital, pharmacy, ATM, grocery and police station, with distances.

      The legend at the bottom left explains the colours; the readout at the bottom right shows the coordinates under the cursor.
